Privacy Policy

App: Cave Copter · Effective date: July 31, 2026

Cave Copter ("the App") takes your privacy seriously. The App does not require sign-up or login and does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous ranking data: To provide the global leaderboard, a random device identifier generated by the App on first launch (not the advertising identifier (IDFA), and not linked to any account or identity) and your best flight distance (m) are sent to and stored on our ranking server. The identifier is discarded when you delete the App, and the raw identifier is never shown to other users.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device information, coarse location, and ad interaction data to serve non-personalized ads. The App does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and does not track you across apps and websites.
  • On-device data: Best score, play tickets, language preference, update-prompt history, and Pro purchase status are stored only on your device.
  • In-app purchase: Payment for the Pro purchase (removes ads, unlimited play) is processed by the Apple App Store. The App does not collect or store any payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Providing the anonymous global leaderboard (best-distance rankings)
  • App features such as saving your best score, keeping language settings, and update notices
  • Verifying Pro purchase status (ad removal, unlimited play)
  • Serving non-personalized ads and operating the App

3. Retention

On-device data is removed when you delete the App. Anonymous leaderboard records (random identifier and best distance) are retained for as long as the leaderboard service operates; you may request deletion via the contact below.

4. Third Parties

  • Google AdMob: Non-personalized advertising. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store: In-app purchase processing. See the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Storage infrastructure for the anonymous ranking data described in Section 1.

5. Your Rights

The App does not collect directly identifying personal information. You can remove on-device data by deleting the App, and you may request deletion of your anonymous leaderboard record via the contact below.

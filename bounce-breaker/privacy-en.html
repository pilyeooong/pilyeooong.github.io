Privacy Policy

App: Bounce Breaker · Effective: July 31, 2026 · 한국어

Bounce Breaker ("the App") does not require sign-up or login, and does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous leaderboard data: To provide the global rankings feature, the App stores a random device identifier generated by the App on first launch (not the advertising identifier IDFA, and not linked to any account) together with your best score on the App's leaderboard server (Supabase). This identifier cannot identify you personally, and only an anonymized tag is shown on the leaderboard.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device information, coarse location, and ad interaction data to serve non-personalized ads. The App does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • On-device data: Best score, play tickets, language preference, update-prompt history, and Pro purchase status are stored only on your device.
  • In-app purchase: Payment for the Pro purchase (ad removal & unlimited plays) is processed by the Apple App Store. The App does not collect or store any payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Providing the anonymous global leaderboard (best score and rank)
  • Providing app features such as best-score saving, language preference, and update notices
  • Verifying Pro purchase status (ad removal & unlimited plays)
  • Serving non-personalized ads and operating the App

3. Retention

On-device data is removed when you delete the App. The device identifier and best score stored on the leaderboard server are retained while the leaderboard service is provided and are deleted upon request via the contact below. Reinstalling the App generates a new identifier that is not linked to previous records.

4. Third Parties

  • Supabase: stores anonymous leaderboard data (device identifier and best score). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: serves non-personalized ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: processes in-app purchases. Apple Privacy Policy

5. Your Rights

The App does not collect directly identifying personal information. You can remove on-device data by deleting the App, and you can request deletion of leaderboard records via the contact below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

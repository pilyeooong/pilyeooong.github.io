Privacy Policy

Boilmark · Effective date: September 25, 2026

Boilmark (the “App”) is a cooking guidance app provided by pilyeooong that reads your device’s barometric pressure sensor to estimate the local boiling point of water and adjust cooking guidance. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information handled by the App

The App does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or cooking content.

  • Sensor data: the App reads your device’s barometric pressure sensor to compute the local boiling point of water in real time. This reading is processed entirely on your device and is never stored, logged, or transmitted anywhere, including to the developer.
  • On-device settings: units, manual elevation entry, language, haptics preference, custom timer names and minutes, purchase entitlement flag, and update-prompt history are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this data.
  • Notification data: if you allow it, the App schedules local notifications on your device to alert you when a kitchen timer finishes. These notifications are generated and delivered entirely on-device.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse location inferred from network information, ad impressions and interactions, product interaction, performance, crash, and diagnostic data to deliver non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, measure performance, and operate its advertising service.
  • Purchase data: the optional one-time “Chef’s Pass” purchase is processed by Apple. The App receives purchase status needed to unlock features but does not receive or store your payment card or billing details.

2. Purposes

  • Compute the local boiling point and adjust cooking timers and guidance
  • Alert you when a kitchen timer finishes, if notifications are allowed
  • Save local settings and preferences
  • Confirm and restore the Chef’s Pass entitlement
  • Provide non-personalized advertising to users without Chef’s Pass
  • Measure ad delivery, diagnose failures, and prevent invalid traffic

3. Advertising and tracking

The App requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. The developer does not use App data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Google may process advertising and diagnostic information under its own terms. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising policies.

4. Third-party services and disclosure

  • Google AdMob: non-personalized advertising, ad measurement, security, and diagnostics.
  • Apple App Store: purchase processing and restoration under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

The developer does not sell personal information and does not provide App data to unrelated third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Barometric pressure readings are never stored, on your device or otherwise — they exist only for the instant it takes to compute the boiling point. Local settings remain on your device until you delete the App or clear its data. Information processed by Google or Apple is retained according to each provider’s policies.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Your rights

You may contact the developer to ask about this policy. Because the App has no account system and the developer does not receive your sensor readings or local settings, the developer generally cannot identify or retrieve a particular user’s local records.

8. Security and international processing

The App minimizes data use by processing sensor readings on-device and keeping settings local. Third-party providers may process information in countries where they operate, using safeguards described in their respective policies.

9. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Privacy Policy

App: Sealed Lot · Effective date: October 3, 2026

Sealed Lot ("the App") respects your privacy. The App does not require sign-up or sign-in and does not collect directly identifiable information such as your name or email address.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous leaderboard data: The profit of your first round of the daily lots and its date (UTC), your best free-play profit, and a random anonymous device identifier generated by the App. This identifier is not linked to an account, an email address, or an advertising identifier, and it is never shown to other players — the standings display only a shortened, non-reversible tag derived from it.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device information, approximate location, ad impressions, clicks, and related advertising data to serve non-personalized ads. The App does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • On-device data: Your daily results, best haul, streak, language setting, and update prompt history are stored on your device only.
  • In-app purchase: The optional Private Room purchase is processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To calculate and display the anonymous daily and free-play standings
  • To provide app functionality such as saved results, streaks, language settings, and update prompts
  • To serve non-personalized ads and operate the App
  • To unlock Private Room features after a purchase is confirmed by the App Store

3. Retention

Anonymous leaderboard data is retained while the service is operated. On-device data and the anonymous identifier are removed from your device when you delete the App.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Provides non-personalized advertising. Google processes data according to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Infrastructure that stores the anonymous standings. Leaderboard rows are readable only through restricted server functions that never return the raw device identifier.
  • Apple App Store: Processes in-app purchases according to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights

The App does not collect directly identifiable account information. You can remove all on-device data by deleting the App. To ask about or request deletion of anonymous leaderboard data, contact the developer using the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.

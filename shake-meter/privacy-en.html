Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

"Shake Meter - Hand Tremor Test" (the "App") values your privacy and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information Collected

The App does not require sign-up or login and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, or phone number.

Motion sensor access: The App uses your device accelerometer to calculate a hand stability score during a short test. Motion readings are processed on your device only and are not recorded, stored on a server, or transmitted to us.

Local storage: The App stores your selected language, personal best score, whether it has shown the update notice and the rating request, and the random identifier used for usage statistics locally on your device. This data remains on your device and is removed when the App is deleted.

Anonymous usage statistics: Anonymous usage statistics are the only data the App sends to our server: measurements finished (test or practice, and whether it set a new personal best), ads shown, and responses to the update notice, together with a random identifier created by the App (not your advertising ID and not linked to any account). Motion readings and scores are not included. They are stored with Supabase, our database hosting provider, which processes them on our behalf; we do not provide them to any other third party.

In addition, the following information may be automatically collected by a third party (Google AdMob) for advertising purposes:

  • Device identifiers used for advertising
  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • App usage data related to ads (ad impressions, clicks, etc.)
  • IP address (for approximate location estimation)

2. Purpose of Use

  • Measuring hand stability using accelerometer readings
  • Saving language preference and personal best score on the device
  • Providing non-personalized advertisements
  • Measuring ad performance and improving the service
  • Understanding how the App is used (anonymous usage statistics)

3. Third-Party Sharing

The App uses the Google AdMob SDK to display banner, native, medium rectangle (300×250), interstitial, and app open ads. The App requests non-personalized ads only and does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency). For details on Google's data collection and use, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not sell or directly provide your motion test results to third parties. Anonymous usage statistics are stored by Supabase (database hosting) on our behalf and are not provided to anyone else.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not store motion sensor readings or scores on any server. Local data (language preference, personal best score, update notice and rating request records, and the statistics identifier) is stored only on your device and is automatically removed when the App is deleted. Raw anonymous usage records are deleted automatically after 30 days; after that we keep only daily totals (event and device counts) with no identifiers. Because they are tied only to a random identifier, we cannot find or delete the records of a specific person; after you delete the App, new statistics are no longer connected to the earlier ones. Data collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's data retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be announced on this page.

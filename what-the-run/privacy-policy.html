What the Run? — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026 · 한국어

This policy covers the mobile game What the Run? ("the App") for iOS and Android, published by MAXLAB. The App has no accounts and no sign-in, and it never asks for your name, e-mail address or contacts.

1. What is processed

ItemWhere it goesWhyHow long
A random device identifier made by the App (not an account and not an advertising ID)The App's ranking table, hosted on SupabaseThe anonymous weekly and all-time rankingAll-time rows: while the ranking is operated. Weekly rows: deleted automatically 8 weeks after their week ends.
Run records: distance, running time, gates cleared, the character and its level, and the time of the recordThe same tableWorking out and showing the ranking; filtering out manipulated valuesSame as above
Advertising data collected by the ad SDK: device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address (used to estimate approximate location), ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic informationGoogle AdMobShowing non-personalized ads, limiting how often an ad is shown, and preventing fraudAccording to Google's policy; the App keeps none of it

What other players can see. On the ranking, a row shows the distance, the character, its level and a four-character anonymous tag derived from the device identifier. The identifier itself is never shown.

Kept on your device. Game progress (best distance, character levels and unlocks, totals), settings (sound, haptics, language), the random identifier above, a ranking record waiting to be sent, when a rating or an update notice was last shown, and a log of the last 50 runs (gates, lost lights). These stay on the device and are removed when you delete the App.

Checking for a new version. At launch the App looks up the latest published version (the App Store's public lookup on iOS; a version table on Supabase on Android). The request carries no device or user value.

Ratings. A rating request is the operating system's own sheet. The App receives nothing you enter there.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. It requests non-personalized ads only and does not track you across apps or websites; it does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT). Google processes the advertising data listed above as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How the information is used

  • To provide the anonymous ranking and show your place on it
  • To keep the ranking fair by refusing values outside what the game allows
  • To show non-personalized ads, cap their frequency and prevent ad fraud
  • To tell you when a newer version of the App is available

4. Third parties

  • Supabase — database hosting for the ranking table and the version table
  • Google AdMob — advertising (Google Privacy Policy)

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your ranking rows. E-mail the address below with the four-character tag shown on your row of the ranking. The rows of that tag are deleted from the server.
  • Remove on-device data. Delete the App. A new random identifier is made if you install it again.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your device's advertising ID in the system settings.
  • You may also ask what is held about your tag, or object to its processing, at the same address.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13.

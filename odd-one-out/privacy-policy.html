Privacy Policy for Find Odd One

Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application “Find Odd One” provided by pilyeooong.

2. Personal Information We Collect

Find Odd One does not require account registration and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

The app stores gameplay records such as score, mistakes, grid size, accuracy, play time, and the time taken for each type of difference (color, shape, angle, size) on your device. This history (the list of your past tests) is kept only on your device and is not transmitted to our server; only the anonymous usage statistics described below leave the device. The app also stores on your device whether it has shown the update notice and the rating request, and the random identifier used for usage statistics.

Anonymous usage statistics are the only data the app sends to our server: screens opened, rounds finished (with that round’s score, misses, and grid size), pauses, ads shown, and a random identifier created by the app (not your advertising ID and not linked to any account). They are stored with Supabase, our database hosting provider.

3. Advertising

Find Odd One uses Google AdMob to display banner, native, medium rectangle (300×250), interstitial, app open, and rewarded ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, approximate location, ad interaction data, diagnostics, and similar information according to Google’s policies. The app requests non-personalized ads only and does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency).

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Purpose of Use

  • To provide the puzzle test, practice mode, and local history features.
  • To display and measure advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • To understand how the app is used (anonymous usage statistics) and improve it.
  • To maintain app stability and basic functionality.

5. Retention

Local gameplay records remain on your device until you delete the app or clear app data. Anonymous usage statistics are kept while the service is operated and are not deleted automatically. Because they are tied only to a random identifier, we cannot find or delete the records of a specific person; after you delete the app, new statistics are no longer connected to the earlier ones. Advertising-related data is handled by Google according to Google’s retention policies.

6. Third-Party Provision and Processing

We do not sell or provide personal information to third parties. Advertising data may be processed by Google AdMob as described above. Anonymous usage statistics are stored by Supabase (database hosting) on our behalf and are not provided to anyone else.

7. Your Rights

You may delete locally stored gameplay records by deleting the app or clearing its data on your device. For advertising data handled by Google, please refer to Google’s privacy controls.

8. Children

Find Odd One is a simple puzzle app and is not directed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

9. Contact

Privacy contact: pilyeooong@gmail.com

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be updated when a material change is made.

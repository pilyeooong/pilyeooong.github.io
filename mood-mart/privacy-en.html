Privacy Policy

Mood Mart · Effective date: September 13, 2026

Mood Mart (the “App”) is a fictional shopping entertainment app provided by pilyeooong. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information handled by the App

The App does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or receipt content.

  • On-device data: onboarding status, language and haptic preferences, fictional receipt history, order counters, and secret-shelf status are stored locally on your device. The developer does not receive this data.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse location inferred from network information, ad impressions and interactions, product interaction, performance, crash, and diagnostic data to deliver non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, measure performance, and operate its advertising service.
  • Shared receipt content: when you choose Share, iOS presents its system share sheet. Content is sent only to the destination you select; the developer does not receive it.

2. Purposes

  • Save fictional receipts and preferences on your device
  • Provide the App's core shopping and sharing features
  • Provide non-personalized advertising
  • Measure ad delivery, diagnose failures, and prevent invalid traffic

3. Advertising, consent, and tracking

The App requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before ads are requested and may provide an advertising privacy-options entry point. The developer does not use App data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Google may process advertising and diagnostic information under its own terms. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising policies.

4. Third-party services and disclosure

The App uses Google AdMob for non-personalized advertising, ad measurement, security, and diagnostics. The developer does not sell personal information and does not provide App data to unrelated third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

Local App data remains on your device until you clear receipt history where available, delete the App, or erase its data. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies. Because the App has no account, deleting the App removes the local data controlled by the App.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed specifically to children under 13 and does not knowingly request personal information from children.

7. Your rights

You may contact the developer to ask about this policy. Where the advertising privacy-options entry point is required, you can use it to review or change your choices. Because the App has no account and the developer does not receive your local records, the developer generally cannot identify or retrieve a particular user's local data.

8. Security and international processing

The App minimizes data use by keeping receipts and preferences on-device. Google may process information in countries where it operates using safeguards described in its policies.

9. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

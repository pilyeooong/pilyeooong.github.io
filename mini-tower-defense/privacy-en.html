Privacy Policy

App: Tiny Bastion · Effective date: August 24, 2026

Tiny Bastion ("the App") respects your privacy. The App does not require an account. The developer does not directly collect names, email addresses, payment details, or other information that directly identifies you. The third-party services described below may process limited device and usage data to provide advertising, diagnostics, and purchases.

1. Information Processed

  • Advertising and diagnostics data: Google AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse location inferred from an IP address, advertising data, product interaction, crash data, performance data, and other technical diagnostic data. Tiny Bastion requests non-personalized ads, does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not use this data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • On-device data: Campaign progress, stars, unlock state, daily records, language and sound settings, and a Pro entitlement cache are stored on your device.
  • In-app purchase: The optional Pro purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive or store payment card or billing information. The App checks the App Store purchase record to unlock and restore Pro.

2. Purposes of Processing

  • Provide non-personalized advertising and support operation of the App.
  • Measure ad and app performance, diagnose crashes, prevent abuse, and maintain reliability.
  • Save game progress and settings and provide purchased Pro functionality.

3. Retention

On-device data remains until you delete the App or clear its data. Google and Apple retain information according to their own privacy policies and legal obligations. The developer does not maintain a separate user profile or server-side gameplay database for the App.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Provides non-personalized ads and related measurement and diagnostics. Google processes information under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store: Processes purchases and purchase restoration under Apple's Privacy Policy.

The developer does not sell personal information. Information processed by third-party services is limited to the purposes described above.

5. Your Choices and Rights

Where required, the App presents Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can revisit Google's ad privacy choices from the in-app Settings screen when the option is available. You may remove locally stored data by deleting the App and may restore an eligible purchase from the Paywall or Settings. Contact the developer below to exercise applicable privacy rights or ask a privacy question.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect directly identifying personal information from children. Advertising requests are non-personalized.

7. International Processing

Google and Apple may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their privacy policies and applicable safeguards.

8. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

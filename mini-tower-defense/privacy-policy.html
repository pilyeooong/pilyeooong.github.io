Privacy Policy

App: Tiny Bastion · Effective date: August 23, 2026

Tiny Bastion ("the App") respects your privacy. The App does not require an account and the developer does not collect personal information.

1. Information Processed by the App

  • On-device data: Campaign progress, stars, unlock state, daily records, language, sound settings, and Pro entitlement cache are stored only on your device.
  • In-app purchase: The optional Pro purchase is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive or store payment card or billing information. The App checks the App Store purchase record to unlock and restore Pro.

2. Purpose

On-device data is used only to provide saved progress, settings, daily records, and purchased Pro functionality.

3. Retention

On-device data remains until you delete the App or clear its data. Apple retains purchase records according to Apple's own policies so that purchases can be restored.

4. Third-Party Provision and Processing

The developer does not sell or provide personal information to third parties and does not outsource personal-data processing. Apple processes App Store purchases under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You may remove locally stored data by deleting the App. You may restore an eligible purchase from the Paywall or Settings. For privacy inquiries, contact the developer below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

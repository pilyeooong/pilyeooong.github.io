Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026 (last updated: October 23, 2026)

"Aim Trainer" (the "App") values your privacy and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information Collected

The App does not require sign-up or login and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, or phone number.

However, the following information may be automatically collected by third parties for advertising purposes: Google AdMob and, on Android, Meta Audience Network, which AdMob may request ads from on our behalf:

  • Device and app identifiers used by the advertising SDK (including the Android advertising ID)
  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • Advertising and app interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, app launches, etc.)
  • Diagnostic and performance data
  • IP address (for approximate location estimation)

For the anonymous global rankings feature, the App collects the following and stores it on a server:

  • A random identifier the App generates for itself on first launch (not your Apple ID or Google account, not the advertising identifier, and not linked to any account); it is also used for ranked duel points and matchmaking
  • Your best score, accuracy, average reaction time and reflex score per game mode
  • Your best run per day (UTC), for the “today” boards
  • A country code — your device’s region setting, or the country you choose in Settings (no location data is used)

This identifier is never displayed to other users; leaderboards and ranked duels show the 4-character tag derived from it and the country code (for example, MX), and, if you create a profile, the name and icon you chose from fixed lists. You can change the country in Settings at any time.

For ranked duels (Ranked 1v1), the App also sends the following to the same server, keyed by the same random identifier — including for ranked Classic runs played outside a duel:

  • Records of your ranked Classic runs: the target layout number, the time of each hit, which target each hit landed on, hit count, accuracy and reflex score
  • Your ranked duel points and record (wins, losses and draws)

If you create a profile, the App also stores, keyed by the same random identifier on the same server, the numbers of the name (two words), icon and color you chose from fixed lists and the time you last changed them. There is no text you type in. Nothing is stored if you do not create a profile.

Anonymous usage statistics: the App also sends a few usage events to the same server — app opens, finished rounds (game mode, score and whether it beat your previous run or best, and in Sweet Spot mode the head and body tap counts), whether a Sweet Spot ranking submission succeeded, ranked duel matches and results (league and duel points, win or loss, opponent hit counts, matchmaking wait time and its steps (connection time, retries and failures)), profile edits (which parts changed), display theme changes, views of the Pro screen, completed purchases (the product only, never payment details) and ad impressions (and how long each full-screen ad stayed open) — together with the platform (iOS or Android), the app version and a separate random identifier the App generates for this purpose. These events are not linked to you, to any account or to the advertising identifier.

Ad measurement (Google Analytics for Firebase): to measure installs and in-app actions that come from our own ads on Google Ads, the App uses Google's Firebase SDK. It sends Google an app-instance identifier the SDK creates for this app; on Android, the Android advertising ID; app opens (including the first open after installation), sessions and app updates; whether a newly installed copy of the App has finished its third round; in-app purchase events the SDK records automatically (product and price, never payment details); device model and OS version; and an approximate location Google derives from your IP address. On iOS the App does not request tracking permission (ATT), so the advertising identifier is not available, and the SDK's collection of the identifier for vendor (IDFV) and of ad personalization signals is turned off. These data are not linked to the random identifiers the App uses for rankings, duels and usage statistics. If you have Pro, the App does not send data to Google Analytics for Firebase; it stops when you buy or restore Pro.

The App stores your full run history, per-mode best scores, and Pro entitlement status on your device. Records of your ranked Classic runs (target layout number, hit times, which target each hit landed on, hit count, accuracy and reflex score) are also sent to the server to provide ranked duel opponents and are kept there for 30 days. In-app purchase transactions are processed by Apple through the App Store (iOS) or by Google through Google Play (Android); the App does not receive or store your payment details.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Providing advertisements (including personalized ads on Android; non-personalized ads only on iOS)
  • Measuring ad performance and improving the service
  • Measuring the effectiveness of our own ads on Google Ads (installs and in-app actions) through Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Operating anonymous global, daily and per-country rankings (best runs per mode and country code, keyed by a random device identifier)
  • Understanding how the App is used, through anonymous usage statistics, to improve it
  • Providing ranked duel opponents: records of your ranked Classic runs are shown to other users as duel opponents together with a 4-character tag and country code, and during a duel the hits from that run are drawn again on the other player's screen
  • Operating ranked duels (so you are not given the same target layout twice, and to calculate duel points), keyed by the random device identifier
  • Ranked duel leaderboard: if you have played 5 or more ranked duels and dueled within the last 30 days, your duel points and league are shown to other users on a leaderboard together with your 4-character tag
  • Showing your profile: if you create one, your name and icon are shown to other users on leaderboards and in ranked duels
  • Storing run history, best scores, and Pro entitlement status (on your device; records of ranked Classic runs are kept on the server for 30 days to provide ranked duel opponents)

3. Third-Party Sharing

The App uses the Google AdMob SDK to display ads. For details on Google's data collection and use, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

On Android, the App uses Google AdMob mediation, so ads may also be served by Meta Audience Network (Meta Privacy Policy). Meta receives the data listed in Section 1 through its SDK.

The App uses Google Analytics for Firebase (Google) to measure our own advertising; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Anonymous ranking data (random device identifier, best runs, country code), ranked duel data (records of ranked Classic runs, duel results, duel points and record), the profile and anonymous usage statistics are stored using Supabase, a cloud database service, solely to operate the rankings and ranked duel features and to analyze how the App is used. See the Supabase Privacy Policy for details.

4. Advertising Privacy Choices

The App checks regional advertising privacy requirements through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Your advertising privacy choices depend on your platform and region:

  • Android: ad requests are not limited to non-personalized ads, so Google may show interest-based (personalized) ads using the Android advertising ID and other data. In your device's ads settings you can delete or reset the advertising ID or, on some devices, turn off personalized ads (opt out of ads personalization). Google does not show personalized ads to users whose Google Account is known to be under 18. Google Analytics for Firebase also uses the advertising ID to attribute in-app actions to our Google Ads campaigns; resetting or deleting the advertising ID applies to it too.
  • Certain US states: if a US state privacy law applies to you, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, or change that choice, under "Ad privacy choices" in the App's Settings. Where the App offers these choices, Google Analytics for Firebase is set not to use your data for advertising measurement, whatever you choose; it is used for analytics only. Your choice also applies to ads served through AdMob mediation by Meta Audience Network.
  • European Economic Area (EEA), UK and Switzerland: the App does not show an ad consent screen in these regions. Under Google's policies, users there are not shown personalized ads; they may be shown only non-personalized or limited ads. Firebase is set not to use your data for advertising in these regions; it is used for analytics only.
  • iOS: ads are requested as non-personalized ads only, and the App does not request tracking permission (ATT). Google may still use device identifiers for advertising measurement and analytics, which Apple may classify as tracking.

If you have Pro, the App does not send data to Google Analytics for Firebase; it stops when you buy or restore Pro.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored on your device (run history, best scores, Pro entitlement) is automatically removed when the App is deleted. Records of ranked Classic runs and duel records sent to the server are deleted once they are 30 days old, regardless of whether the App is deleted; ranked duel points and record are kept while the ranked duel feature is operated, and you may request their deletion with your 4-character tag in the same way as described below. The profile is kept while the feature is operated, and you may request its deletion with your 4-character tag in the same way as ranking records. Anonymous ranking records (random identifier, best runs, country code) are retained on the server to operate the leaderboards, and per-day records for the “today” boards are deleted after 30 days; because the identifier is not linked to you, we cannot look it up from any personal information, but you may request deletion by contacting the address below with the 4-character tag shown next to your entry. Anonymous usage events are kept on the server to analyze how the App is used; they are linked only to a random identifier and cannot be looked up from any personal information. Data collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's data retention policies. Data collected by Meta Audience Network is governed by Meta's own data retention policies. Data collected by Google Analytics for Firebase is kept at the event level for 2 months (our retention setting) and in aggregated reports under Google's policies. It is linked only to an identifier the SDK creates, so we cannot look it up from any personal information or delete it on request; on Android you can reset the advertising ID in your device settings. Sending stops when you buy or restore Pro.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

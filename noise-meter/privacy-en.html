Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 2, 2026 · Last updated: October 27, 2026

"Noise Meter - dB Sound Level" (the "App") values your privacy and complies with applicable laws. This policy explains what information is collected and how it is used.

1. Information Collected

The App does not require sign-up or login and does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as name, email, or phone number.

Microphone access: The App accesses your device microphone to measure ambient sound levels in real time. Audio input is used solely to calculate dB readings within the App and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

Anonymous usage statistics: The App sends anonymous usage statistics (screens opened, how many readings and how long they ran, noise level bands, ads shown, and a random identifier) to Supabase, a database hosting service that stores them on our behalf. Recordings and sound are never sent. This data is not provided to any other third party.

The following information may be automatically collected by a third party (Google AdMob) for advertising purposes:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID)
  • Device information (device model, OS version)
  • App usage data (ad impressions, clicks, etc.)
  • IP address (for approximate location estimation)

The App stores the following on your device only: saved readings (time, length, average and peak dB of the last 50 readings), your language, update notice and rating request records, and the random identifier used for usage statistics.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Real-time ambient noise level (dB) measurement via microphone input
  • Providing non-personalized ads only (banner, interstitial, native, app open and rewarded formats); the App does not ask for tracking permission
  • Measuring ad performance and improving the service
  • Keeping saved readings on your device
  • Understanding how the App is used through anonymous usage statistics

3. Third-Party Sharing

Anonymous usage statistics are stored by Supabase (processing on our behalf). The App uses the Google AdMob SDK to display ads. For details on Google's data collection and use, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

The App does not store audio data on any server. Anonymous usage statistics are kept for as long as the service is operated and are not deleted automatically; because they are linked only to a random identifier, individual deletion requests cannot be matched to a person. Deleting the App removes the identifier from your device. Data stored on your device is removed when the App is deleted, and saved readings can also be removed with "Clear all". Data collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's data retention policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be announced on this page.

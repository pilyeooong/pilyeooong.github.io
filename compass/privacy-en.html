Privacy Policy

App Name: Compass - Magnetic North

Developer: pilyeooong

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

1. Personal Information Collection

This app does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, contact details, or location. No account registration, sign-in, or contact details are required.

2. Location and Sensors

This app determines direction using your device's magnetometer (motion sensor) only. Sensor readings are processed on your device in real time to show the heading and are never stored or transmitted.

The app does not use location services (GPS) and does not request location permission.

3. Local Data Storage

The app stores the following information locally on your device only (using AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device:

  • Your selected language preference
  • A record of which update notices have been shown
  • A random identifier used for anonymous usage statistics (see Section 4)

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

4. Anonymous Usage Statistics

To improve the app, it records anonymous usage statistics (screens viewed, ad impressions, and the number of times the compass is used) together with a randomly generated identifier, the app version, and the device time. The data is stored in Supabase, a database hosting service (data processing is entrusted to Supabase), and is not provided to any other third party. It does not include your name, contact details, or location.

The data is kept for as long as the service operates, and there is no automatic deletion. Because the identifier is random and not linked to you, we cannot process requests to delete individual records. Uninstalling the app removes the identifier from your device, so later records are no longer connected to it.

5. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display banner, native, interstitial and app open ads. AdMob may collect device-level data such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android)
  • IP address, device type, and operating system version
  • General location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected by Google and used in accordance with Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The app requests non-personalized ads only and does not ask for tracking permission.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements
  • Supabase — for storing anonymous usage statistics (data processing entrusted)

8. Data Security

The app does not collect or transmit personally identifiable information. The only data sent to our server is the anonymous usage statistics described in Section 4, which are not linked to your identity.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the developer at pilyeooong@gmail.com.

10. Policy Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. The latest version is always available at this URL.

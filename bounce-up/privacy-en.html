Privacy Policy

App: Bounce Up · Effective date: July 27, 2026

Bounce Up ("the App") respects your privacy. The App does not require sign-up or login, and does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name or email address.

1. Information We Process

  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device information, approximate location, and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) to serve non-personalized ads. The App does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and does not track you across apps and websites.
  • On-device data: Your best score, language preference, update-prompt history, and Pro purchase status are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our servers.
  • In-app purchases: Payment for the Pro upgrade (ad removal) is processed entirely by the Apple App Store. The App does not collect or store any payment information.

2. How We Use Information

  • Saving your best score, keeping your language preference, and showing update notices
  • Verifying your Pro purchase status (to remove ads)
  • Serving non-personalized ads and operating the App

3. Data Retention

On-device data is removed when you delete the App. The App does not operate its own servers and does not retain user data server-side.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: serves non-personalized ads. Google's data handling is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store: processes in-app purchases. Apple's data handling is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights

The App does not collect directly identifying personal information. You can remove all on-device data by deleting the App. For any privacy inquiries, contact us using the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.

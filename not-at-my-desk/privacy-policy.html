Privacy Policy

App: Not at My Desk · Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy applies to the Not at My Desk app ("the App"). The App does not require sign-up or sign-in and does not collect directly identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

1. Information We Collect

  • Anonymous usage statistics: The App sends anonymous usage events to understand how it is used — for example app opens, how long the walking screen was viewed, opening the "while you were away" card, flipping or sharing a postcard, using Photo mode and attempting to save, whether a home screen widget is placed or tapped, notification permission answers, and ad impressions. Each event carries the platform, app version, device time, and a random identifier the App creates on first launch only for this purpose. This identifier is not your advertising identifier, is not linked to an account, and cannot identify you.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process device information, the advertising identifier, approximate location derived from the IP address, ad impressions and clicks, and diagnostic data to serve ads. The App requests non-personalized ads only. It does not use App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Content downloads: The App downloads text and images (lines, postcards, seasonal items) from our server. These requests include only a language code and no device identifier or user information. As with any internet request, your IP address reaches the server host (Supabase); the App does not store or use it to identify you, and it is handled under Supabase's standard logging.
  • On-device data: Your walking progress, collected souvenirs, postcards, lines, settings, and prompt history are stored on your device. On iOS, progress is also kept in your own iCloud (key-value storage) if iCloud is turned on, so it can be restored on the same Apple ID. We cannot access your device data or your iCloud.
  • Photos: When you tap save in Photo mode or on a postcard, the image is saved to your Photos with add-only permission. The App does not read your photo library and does not upload these images.
  • Notifications and widgets: Postcard notifications are scheduled locally on your device, and widgets are drawn from data on your device. Nothing is sent for these features.

2. How We Use Information

  • To understand and improve how the App is used (anonymous usage statistics)
  • To serve non-personalized ads, which fund the App
  • To show lines and postcards downloaded from our server
  • To keep and restore your progress

3. Retention and Deletion

Anonymous usage statistics are kept while the App is offered and are deleted when the App is withdrawn from sale. Because they are linked only to a random identifier, we cannot find or delete a specific person's records on request. Data on your device, including the random identifier, is removed when you delete the App. Progress in iCloud can be removed in your device's iCloud settings. Data processed by Google AdMob follows Google's policies.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob: Provides non-personalized ads. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Hosts the database that stores anonymous usage statistics (as a processor) and serves the App's text and images.
  • Apple: iCloud storage of your progress and the App Store version check are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell or provide your information to any other third party.

5. Your Choices

You can delete all on-device data by deleting the App, turn off notifications in your device settings, and remove saved images from your Photos at any time. For questions about your data, contact us at the address below.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
